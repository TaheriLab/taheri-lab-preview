# Research projects

The Taheri Lab develops machine learning methods to study cancer
biology, predict drug interactions, and identify opportunities
for drug repurposing. Our work combines biological knowledge
with molecular data to investigate complex biomedical questions.


{% include section.html %}

## Cancer biology and multi-omics

We develop machine learning methods to identify genes, regulatory
networks, and molecular pathways involved in cancer development,
progression, and recurrence.

Our research integrates single-cell and other multi-omics data
with biological networks to study differences between tumors
and identify molecular patterns associated with clinical outcomes.

Current projects include breast cancer gene regulation, recurrence
in advanced ovarian cancer, and metastatic behavior in lung cancer.

{% include section.html %}

## Drug interactions, treatment effects, and repurposing

We develop machine learning methods to predict drug–drug
interactions and investigate their biological mechanisms.

By combining information about drugs, molecular targets,
biological pathways, and adverse effects, we aim to improve
drug interaction prediction and identify potential new uses
for existing drugs.

We also analyze cancer cell–fibroblast co-cultures at single-cell
resolution to study how drug treatments affect the spatial
organization of these cell populations.

We collaborate with experimental and clinical researchers at
Karolinska Institutet (KI) on both our cancer biology and
drug research projects.

## Computational methods

Across these research areas, we develop graph-based and multimodal
machine learning methods, integrate biomedical knowledge graphs,
and investigate how biological knowledge can improve predictions
and their interpretation.
